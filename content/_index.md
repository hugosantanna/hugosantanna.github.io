I am an applied labor economist at the [Collat School of Business](https://www.uab.edu/business/home/), University of Alabama at Birmingham. I am interested in how large-scale disruptions, such as immigration, reshape labor markets and firm ownership. I also work on difference-in-differences methods when time-varying covariates carry the identification.

In my spare time, I build [AI-assisted scaffolds](https://hsantanna.org/clo-author/) for research and teaching.

I received my PhD in Economics from the University of Georgia.
