I am an Assistant Professor at the [Collat School of Business](https://www.uab.edu/business/home/), University of Alabama at Birmingham. My research interests lie at the intersection of Labor Economics, Econometrics, and Causal Inference.

In my spare time, I build [AI-assisted scaffolds](https://hsantanna.org/clo-author/) for research and teaching.

I received my PhD in Economics from the University of Georgia.
